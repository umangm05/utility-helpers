Unit Converter

Unit converter is a free online tool that converts between measurement units for length, weight, temperature, volume, area, speed, and more. Whether you are following a recipe in metric, converting miles to kilometers for a road trip, or checking the temperature in Fahrenheit, this tool gives you instant accurate results. Choose a category, enter your value, select the units, and see the conversion immediately. Everything runs in your browser with no signup, no installation, and no data ever leaving your device.

Frequently asked questions

What unit categories can I convert?

Length, weight, temperature, volume, area, speed, time, and data storage units are all supported.

How accurate are the conversions?

Conversions use internationally standard conversion factors and display results with appropriate precision.

Is there a limit to how many conversions I can do?

No. You can convert as many values as you want for free.
