Timestamp Converter

Timestamp converter is a free online tool that converts Unix timestamps to human-readable dates and back. Unix timestamps count the number of seconds or milliseconds since January 1, 1970, and are used everywhere in computing — APIs, databases, log files, and JavaScript all rely on them. But humans cannot read raw timestamps, so developers use this tool to convert them into readable dates and times. Enter a timestamp to see the date in your local timezone and UTC, or enter a date to get its Unix timestamp equivalent. Everything runs in your browser with no signup and no data leaving your device.

Frequently asked questions

What is a Unix timestamp?

A Unix timestamp is the number of seconds (or milliseconds) that have elapsed since January 1, 1970, also known as the Unix epoch.

Does this tool handle milliseconds?

Yes. The tool automatically detects whether your timestamp is in seconds or milliseconds and converts accordingly.

Can I convert a date to a timestamp?

Yes. Enter a date and time, and the tool will show you the equivalent Unix timestamp in both seconds and milliseconds.
