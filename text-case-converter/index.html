Text Case Converter

Text case converter is a free online tool that transforms text between uppercase, lowercase, sentence case, title case, and camel case. If you have ever pasted text that came through in all caps, or needed to format a headline in title case, this tool fixes it in seconds. Writers use it to clean up formatting, students use it to standardize essay titles, and developers use it to convert variable names between camelCase and snake_case. Simply paste your text, choose the target case format, and copy the result. Everything runs in your browser with no signup and no data leaving your device.

Frequently asked questions

What case formats are supported?

UPPERCASE, lowercase, Sentence case, Title Case, camelCase, PascalCase, and snake_case.

Does it handle long text?

Yes. You can convert paragraphs or entire documents without any length limits.

Is my text saved anywhere?

No. Everything runs in your browser. Your text is never sent to a server.
