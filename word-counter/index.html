Word Counter

Word counter is a free online tool that counts words, characters, sentences, and paragraphs in your text as you type. Whether you are writing an essay for school, crafting a social media post, or optimizing a meta description for SEO, knowing your exact word count helps you stay within platform limits. The tool works entirely in your browser — no signup, no installation, and no data ever leaves your device. Everything runs locally using JavaScript for complete privacy and instant results.

Frequently asked questions

Does this word counter save my text?

No. Everything runs in your browser's memory. When you close or refresh the page, your text is gone.

Can I use this tool offline?

Yes. Once the page loads, the word counter works without an internet connection.

Does it count characters with or without spaces?

Both. You get separate counts for characters including spaces and characters excluding spaces.
