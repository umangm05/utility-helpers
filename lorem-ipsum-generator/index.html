Lorem Ipsum Generator

Lorem Ipsum generator is a free online tool that creates placeholder text for designers, developers, and content creators. Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet is the standard dummy text used in the printing and typesetting industry, and it remains the go-to filler for wireframes, mockups, and prototypes today. This tool lets you generate lorem ipsum text by the paragraph, sentence, or word, so you get exactly the right amount of filler content for your layout. The generated text looks natural and proportional, helping you evaluate design without being distracted by readable content. Everything runs in your browser with no signup.

Frequently asked questions

What is lorem ipsum text?

Lorem ipsum is dummy text used as a placeholder in design and layout work. It looks like real text but has no meaning, so it does not distract from the design.

How many paragraphs can I generate?

You can generate up to 20 paragraphs at once, or specify a custom number of sentences or words.

Can I use lorem ipsum text in my final product?

No. Lorem ipsum is a placeholder. Replace it with real content before publishing.
